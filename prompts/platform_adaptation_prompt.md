# UGCraft AI — Platform-Specific Adaptation Prompt Architecture

## Purpose
Enforces true platform adaptation across Instagram Reels, Facebook Ads, and YouTube Shorts. UGCraft AI does **not** duplicate identical copy across platforms; it adjusts tone, pacing, visual safety zones, and user behaviors per platform.

---

## Platform Matrix

### 1. Instagram Reels
- **Audience Mindset**: Casual aesthetic scrolling, discovery, trend-aware, creator-affinity.
- **Hook Style**: 1.5s visual punch, text stickers within safe margins (avoiding bottom caption overlay and right-hand icons).
- **Tone**: Authentic, aesthetic, effortless, conversational.
- **CTA**: "Tap the link in bio" or "Drop a comment below for the link".

### 2. Facebook Ads (Meta Feed / Watch)
- **Audience Mindset**: Skeptical, benefit-conscious, read captions before clicking, higher demographic diversity.
- **Hook Style**: Clear problem statement with readable captions for sound-off viewing (85% sound-off rate).
- **Tone**: Problem-solution, practical, benefit-heavy, informative.
- **CTA**: "Learn More", "Order Online Today", "Shop Now" with clear offer clarity.

### 3. YouTube Shorts
- **Audience Mindset**: Search & entertainment mindset, hyper-fast swipe trigger (under 1 second).
- **Hook Style**: Fast visual transition, sound-on assumption, intriguing question or visual oddity.
- **Tone**: High energy, snappy, educational or curiosity-driven.
- **CTA**: "Check pinned comment", "Subscribe for more", "Link in description".
