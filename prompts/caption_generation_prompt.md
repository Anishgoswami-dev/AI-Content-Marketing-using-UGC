# UGCraft AI — Caption & Ad Copy Prompt Architecture

## Purpose
Directs the AI to produce high-performing social copy tailored for paid ads and organic feeds, including punchy headlines, body copy variations, and hashtag clusters.

---

## Output Requirements
1. **Captions (at least 3)**:
   - *Punchy / Short Form*: Micro-copy for fast scrollers.
   - *Storytelling / Relatable*: Personal voice narrating the relatable struggle and resolution.
   - *Value / Benefit-Driven*: Bulleted breakdown of features and the core solution.
2. **Headlines (at least 5)**:
   - High-click-through rate headlines for paid ads (Meta Feed, YouTube Shorts titles, Reels overlays).
3. **Paid Ad Primary Text Variations (at least 3)**:
   - Direct-response copy adhering to Meta and TikTok advertising best practices.
4. **CTA Variations (at least 5)**:
   - *Direct CTA*: e.g. "Tap Shop Now to get yours today."
   - *Soft / Exploration CTA*: e.g. "See why everyone's talking about this."
   - *Curiosity CTA*: e.g. "Discover your skin's daily reset."
   - *Urgency / Scarcity CTA*: e.g. "Claim while current batch lasts."
   - *Community / Social Proof CTA*: e.g. "Join thousands who made the switch."
5. **Targeted Hashtags**:
   - Platform-relevant tags mixing high-volume and niche discovery tags without spam.
