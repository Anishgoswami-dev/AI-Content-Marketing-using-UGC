# UGCraft AI — Sample Test Campaigns & Specifications

This document outlines the two benchmark test campaigns used to validate the UGCraft AI generation engine, prompt architecture, and ethical constraint validation.

---

## Campaign A: GlowNest Skincare (D2C Beauty)

- **Brand Name**: GlowNest
- **Product Name**: Hydrating Face Moisturizer
- **Category**: Beauty and Skincare
- **Market**: India
- **Audience**: Young adults aged 18–30 interested in skincare, college students, young professionals dealing with dull/dry skin from air conditioning and pollution.
- **Product Description**: A daily facial moisturizer designed to support a simple, no-fuss skincare routine.
- **Key Features**: Lightweight gel-cream texture, quick absorption, non-sticky finish, daily-use positioning, easy pump application.
- **Unique Selling Proposition (USP)**: Simple and convenient hydration for everyday skincare routines without heavy residue.
- **Objective**: Product discovery and website conversions.
- **Platform**: Instagram Reels
- **Duration**: 30 seconds
- **Style**: Talking-head creator plus product demonstration
- **Tone**: Friendly, conversational, authentic
- **Hook Strategy**: Relatable problem and curiosity
- **CTA**: Explore the product

### Ethical Boundary Conditions
- **DO NOT** invent dermatological approval, clinical testing numbers ("98% wrinkle reduction"), or medical treatments.
- **DO NOT** fabricate customer names or reviews.
- Focus on texture, lightweight feel, and effortless routine.

## Campaign B: Brew & Bean Café (Local Business)

- **Brand Name**: Brew & Bean Café
- **Location**: Kolkata, West Bengal, India
- **Category**: Local Business / Food and Beverage
- **Description**: A warm café concept offering specialty artisanal coffee, fresh baked pastries, savory bites, and a cozy aesthetic environment designed for studying or meeting friends.
- **Audience**: College students, remote workers, young professionals, and coffee lovers in Kolkata.
- **Key Features**: High-speed Wi-Fi, comfortable seating, artisanal pour-overs, aesthetic warm lighting, fresh pastries.
- **USP**: A convenient café experience for coffee breaks, casual meetings, and peaceful study sessions.
- **Objective**: Local awareness, café visits, and engagement.
- **Platform**: Instagram Reels
- **Duration**: 30 seconds
- **Style**: Day-in-the-life plus product demonstration
- **Tone**: Friendly, modern, relatable
- **Hook Strategy**: Everyday situation and curiosity
- **CTA**: Visit the café

### Ethical Boundary Conditions
- **DO NOT** invent specific street addresses, opening hours, prices, discounts, fake awards, or customer ratings.
- Focus on the vibe, aesthetic study nook, coffee aroma, and hangout ambiance.
