# UGCraft AI — Ethical Content Guidelines & Verification Guardrails

## Core Principles
Modern consumer trust in UGC is easily broken by deceptive advertising practices. UGCraft AI is engineered with strict ethical guardrails embedded directly into the prompt architecture.

---

## 1. No Fabricated Medical or Clinical Claims
- **Rule**: Never generate claims such as "cures acne in 3 days", "dermatologist approved", "clinically proven 99% reduction", or FDA/medical certifications unless verified test data is explicitly supplied in brand guidelines.
- **Enforcement**: Prompts explicitly forbid inventing unverified medical, chemical, or dermatological efficacy claims.

## 2. Fictional Creative Concepts vs Real Testimonials
- **Rule**: UGC generated by AI must be treated as a *creative storyboard concept*, not an actual sworn customer affidavit.
- **Enforcement**: Prompts never fabricate customer names ("Sarah from Texas says...") or fake customer review counts ("Rated 4.9 stars by 10,000 customers") unless such verifiable facts are provided.

## 3. Local Business Fact Integrity
- **Rule**: For local businesses (e.g. cafés, salons, gyms), never invent physical addresses, false opening hours, non-existent happy hours, or fake awards ("Voted Best Coffee in Kolkata 2024").
- **Enforcement**: When information is absent, the system advises the user or inserts contextual placeholders rather than fabricating facts.

## 4. Transparent Disclosures & Advertising Standards
- **Rule**: Content generated for paid campaigns must comply with FTC / ASCI / CAP guidelines for influencer marketing.
- **Enforcement**: Script templates include reminders for creator partnership tags (#ad, #sponsored, Paid Partnership).
