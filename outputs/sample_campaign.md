# CAMPAIGN A: GlowNest Skincare (Hydrating Face Moisturizer)
- **Brand**: GlowNest
- **Product**: Hydrating Face Moisturizer
- **Category**: Beauty and Skincare
- **Target Audience**: Young adults aged 18–30, college students, working professionals coping with humid climate and dry skin after washing
- **Platform**: Instagram Reels (9:16 Vertical Video)
- **Duration**: 30 seconds
- **Objective**: Product discovery and website conversions
- **Tone**: Friendly, conversational, authentic creator
- **UGC Style**: Talking-head creator plus product demonstration

---

## 1. Attention-Grabbing Hooks (5 Variations)

### Hook 1 [Common Mistake]
> "If your skin feels tight after washing your face, stop doing this right now."
- **Audience Motivation**: Triggers instant self-correction instinct regarding daily skincare error.
- **Recommended Creative Use**: Creator touches cheek gently with slight grimace directly to selfie camera in natural window light.

### Hook 2 [Relatable Situation]
> "POV: You finally found a moisturizer that doesn’t turn your face into an oil slick by 2 PM."
- **Audience Motivation**: Directly addresses the humid-weather struggle young adults face daily with heavy creams.
- **Recommended Creative Use**: Split screen: creator frustrated with greasy forehead vs smiling tapping lightweight GlowNest bottle.

### Hook 3 [Curiosity]
> "Why is nobody talking about simple 1-step hydration that actually sinks in?"
- **Audience Motivation**: Resonates with consumer fatigue over complicated 10-step skincare routines.
- **Recommended Creative Use**: Creator holding minimal pump bottle close to lens, tapping nails on lid.

### Hook 4 [Product Demonstration]
> "Tell me your moisturizer is lightweight without telling me your moisturizer is lightweight."
- **Audience Motivation**: Uses viral audio format while priming for instant sensory visual proof.
- **Recommended Creative Use**: Extreme close up dispensing a single pump of water-gel cream on back of hand.

### Hook 5 [Unexpected Fact]
> "The only thing standing between you and fresh, non-sticky skin all day is this one habit."
- **Audience Motivation**: Lowers resistance by promising a single micro-habit rather than an expensive overhaul.
- **Recommended Creative Use**: Creator smiling in cozy bathroom setting preparing for work/college.
